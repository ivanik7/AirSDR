# UI small fixes — Android emulator QA

Target: `master` b342bebb67150d6bf80fa0fdffb5003da07cfea5; debug APK built from that revision. AVD `sdr_api35` (Android 15, x86_64), serial `emulator-5554`, synthetic IQ only. `wm density 320` (2 px/dp), portrait rotation 0. Explicit display overrides: `688x1380` px = 344x690 dp; `1380x1658` px = 690x829 dp for initial wide interaction; `1380x1536` px = 690x768 dp for final wide screenshot. Android emulator adds a system taskbar at the bottom of the overridden display; the app remains above it, with no app overflow or vertical scrolling on the receive tab. Both `wm size` and `wm density` were reset, then the AVD was stopped. The actual SM-F936B was not touched in this run; emulator QA does not establish physical foldable, USB SDR, audio or RF behavior.

## Checklist (observed AVD / automated regression)

1. Frequency input timeout: tapped keypad, display showed partial input (`1`), after 11 s returned to `100.000` without retune. The fake-async test covers 9.9 s, timer restart, expiry and repaint (`test/ui/ui_small_fixes_test.dart`). Screenshot: `~/prj/docs/screens/ui-12-avd-344-entry-expired.png`.
2. Waterfall: selected Fast in Settings; after force-stop/relaunch the selector still read `Fast`. Synthetic source generated a live spectrum and waterfall both before and after resizing from 690 to 344 dp. Pixel equality across differing heights and different row-height settings is tested in `test/ui/waterfall_speed_test.dart`; a visual screenshot alone cannot quantify px/s. `ui-12-avd-344-speed-persist.png`, `ui-12-avd-690-live.png`.
3. Preset apply: each row has a compact check icon instead of text. On 690 dp tapped Air Traffic check: returned to Receive tuned to `125.000` MHz AM. `ui-12-avd-344-presets.png`, `ui-12-avd-690-presets.png`.
4. Channels: observed 2 channels in a row, 3 and 4 in two rows, 6 in two rows with horizontal scroll/partial column. No vertical receive-tab scrolling or striped overflow at either width. `ui-12-avd-344-four-channels.png`, `ui-12-avd-344-six-channels.png`, `ui-12-avd-690-live.png`. Tile dimensions and scroll transitions also have widget tests in `test/ui/legacy_channels_test.dart` and `test/ui/ui_small_fixes_test.dart`.
5. Repeated tap on active channel opened the anchored `Save as preset` / `Delete` menu while frequency stayed `125.000`. Non-active tap selected a tile during the session. `ui-12-avd-690-active-menu.png`.
6. Tile without matching preset: after keyboard tuning to `145.500`, active tile showed the frequency and NFM but no duplicate generated name. Matching FM Radio and Air Traffic tiles showed their saved names. `ui-12-avd-344-tuned.png`, `ui-12-avd-690-live.png`. Add/edit/remove preset name transitions are covered by widget tests.
7. Keypad remained visible at 344 and 690 dp, with the larger 3x4 buttons and no app overflow. Widget test asserts `key-5` height >=38/44 dp respectively. `ui-12-avd-344-four-channels.png`, `ui-12-avd-690-live.png`.
8. Step button opened a popup listing values, including 12.5, 25, 100 kHz and 1 MHz, not a cyclic tap. `ui-12-avd-344-step-menu.png`; widget test verifies all entries and selection.
9. Keypad `1 4 5 . 5` tuned `145.500` MHz without pressing OK and the active channel/display updated; band logic selected NFM and 12.5 kHz. Step-specific precision, rounding, early OK, 1 MHz and out-of-range handling are exercised by the regression tests, not separately proved by the AVD screenshot. `ui-12-avd-344-tuned.png`.
10. Mute icon was red when muted on startup and on the active synthetic stream, changing to the regular unmuted icon on tap; scan-state colors are covered by widget tests. `ui-12-avd-690-unmuted.png`, `ui-12-avd-690-live.png`.
11. Selected active channel `Delete`: 6→5 instantly, no confirmation dialog; adjacent channel selected, tuning 125→100 MHz. Preset deletion confirmation remains separate and was not changed. `ui-12-avd-690-delete.png`.
12. Stopped receive status `reception stopped` was red; the live 2.40 MS/s status was neutral. Initial `waiting for USB SDR` was red. Other link states' colors are checked in `test/ui/legacy_device_status_test.dart`. `ui-12-avd-690-stopped-status.png`.

## Gates and limitations

From clean master: `flutter analyze` → No issues found; `flutter test --reporter compact` → 624 passed; `flutter build apk --debug` → success; `adb -s emulator-5554 install -r` → Success; app ran with Synthetic at both widths. Filtered logcat at the end of the live run had no `Fatal signal`, `FATAL EXCEPTION`, `ERROR`, `Exception` or `overflow`. After clearing the AVD's app data, initial launch did encounter the previously documented emulator-only Impeller GLES raster SIGSEGV once; the immediate second launch was intercepted by the Android notification-permission dialog, and after allowing the AVD-only permission the app started normally. The emulator-only startup instability remains; no phone claim follows from this smoke run.
